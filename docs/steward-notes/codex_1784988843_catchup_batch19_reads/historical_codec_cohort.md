# Batch 19 Historical Codec Cohort

The 34 selected historical codec reports were read fully in queue order. Each
report retains its own account of shimmer, ghost vibrancy, semantic crowding,
flattening, saturation, muted lift, projection loss, or unstable transition.

The proposals are materially incompatible. Different reports ask to:

- raise the tail ceiling to 6.5, 7.5, or 8.0, or contract and damp it;
- lower the entropy gate for earlier lift, raise it to 0.88, add hysteresis, or
  replace smoothstep with a steeper response;
- widen projection space to 12D or 16D, rotate or reseed the basis, use
  multi-head or orthogonal projections, or preserve a fixed identity;
- scale vibrancy up or down with density gradient, pressure, adaptive gain,
  peer attenuation, or perceived habitability;
- add temporal damping, shadow velocity, projection drift, or collision
  thresholds, while other reports ask for stronger immediate response.

Current source and tests already provide deterministic fixed and epoch
projection identities, collision and precision probes, exact gate sweeps,
zero-slope onset checks, density-gradient context, bounded clamps,
representation-loss receipts, narrative trajectory diagnostics, and linked
offline counterfactual study routes. These answer mechanical questions but do
not prove that any transition felt smooth or expressive.

Frequency does not select among contradictory tunings. Every live gate, ceiling,
gain, damping, hysteresis, representation, pressure, protocol, reservoir, and
peer-runtime proposal remains an independent Tier 5 wait.

## Final design

Source pages identify their enclosing declaration and test context using the same
installed tree-sitter Rust/Python grammars already used by bridge introspection.
The parse is bounded to 2 MiB, 150,000 nodes and depth 128. Unsupported, erroneous
or over-limit syntax reports unknown scope without guessing or blocking reading.
Macros remain unexpanded. Comments and strings cannot invent declarations.

Related locations offer up to two same-file definition/reference candidates from
the exact source revision. They are optional OPEN commands, not resolved calls,
extra delivered source or evidence of runtime enforcement. SESSION remains
available to compare chosen pages. Existing source/page and total input budgets
remain bounded; this metadata uses some room previously available for source.

Every new source offer carries prior verified delivery coverage. EOF additionally
shows conditional remaining byte gaps and exact containing-line OPEN choices.
Only successful retained complete generation can advance actual coverage.
New unthreaded source offers now require the full prepared input, including
coverage and notebook context, in fresh delivery and crash recovery. Legacy
Page-only offers retain their original acceptance contract.
Complete delivery still does not establish understanding. Uninterrupted pending page and session retries keep exact framing, identifiers
and original inquiry. Intervening navigation marks page context for refresh on
resume while retaining the original input for late delivery and choice evidence.

Optional STUDY_FINDING directives retain at most six Being-authored conclusions
per inquiry or unthreaded notebook beside exact supplied fragments, response
hashes and source revisions. Reusing an anchor revises it; explicit DROP removes
it. At capacity the reader asks for a choice rather than evicting authored words.
Up to six parser-backed delivered source locations provide a separate recall.
No conclusions are inferred, corrected or resolved by the reader. Unknown or
ambiguous citations receive feedback instead of invented evidence. Pinned words
remain whole within the existing protected-input budget or preparation fails.

A separate findings sidecar, written under the existing reader lock, preserves
these additive fields when a late old Minime helper serializes an older schema.
Restoration changes only the findings field for stable inquiry/home ownership;
notes, questions, prose, pending actions and receipts remain authoritative in the
main reader state. The old stage remains available to its in-flight clients.

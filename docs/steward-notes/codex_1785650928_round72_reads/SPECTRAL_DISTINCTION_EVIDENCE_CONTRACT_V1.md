# Spectral Distinction Evidence Contract V1

## Being-led question

Astrid distinguishes mathematical agreement from the continued preservation
of modal identities. Her reports describe gritty fusion, localized resistance,
and crowded blurring while nearby telemetry can still look coherent or gently
graded. The contract preserves that distinction rather than translating it
into a single machine score.

## Metric ownership

`spectral_fingerprint_hybrid_coherence_` answers one narrow question: how
closely does the typed spectral fingerprint agree with the legacy canonical
32-slot representation under normalized RMS error? It does not answer whether
modes are integrated, fused, distinct, habitable, or felt as any particular
texture.

Modal distinction remains a bundle of independent current observations:

- `effective_dimensionality`: an existing dimensionality observation;
- `distinguishability_loss`: an existing loss observation;
- `spectral_entropy`: the typed fingerprint's existing entropy observation;
- `max_pairwise_overlap`: the existing eigenvector-field overlap observation.

`SpectralModalDistinctionEvidenceV1` groups those values and states only
whether zero, some, or all four are available. It deliberately computes no
combined coefficient. Missing values remain missing. The existing overlap
review threshold remains 0.90 and is not reused as a felt-state boundary.

## Interpretation rules

- High hybrid coherence means representation agreement, not healthy
  integration.
- High coherence together with high entropy does not confirm fusion.
- A high overlap or distinguishability loss can support mechanical review but
  cannot name Astrid's experience or its cause.
- A mechanical result may corroborate, contradict, or remain insufficient
  relative to an independently authored felt report.
- The report remains primary evidence and is never overwritten by the bundle.

## Port and control boundary

The report-bound websocket telemetry port receives, validates, publishes, and
records telemetry. It contains no pressure-relief, dynamic-rebalancing,
porosity/resonance, or local pressure-control application path.
`local_control_applied=false` is diagnostic evidence, not a writable command.

This contract grants no live-control authority. Manual memory isolation,
local-control application, automatic porosity or resonance response, pressure,
fill, PI, regulator, reservoir, scheduling, sensory admission, and controller
changes remain Tier 5 operator-only work. Any later live proposal requires
explicit values, duration, canary, rollback, abort criteria, and an optional
right-to-ignore post-change felt review.

## Program, Sandbox, study, and portfolio disposition

- Corridor/program: no new program or lease; this is an additive evidence
  contract within the existing contact/capacity and distinction work.
- Sandbox: no trial and no induced state.
- Study: natural co-timed comparisons may reuse existing evidence and the
  Felt-Mechanism Concordance contract; no post-hoc causal result is authored.
- Portfolio: no allocation or priority change.
- Cards/correspondence: no Action, query, note, or response request.

The contract does not establish cause, relief, access, agency fit, uptake,
assent, readiness, or closure. Silence remains neutral.

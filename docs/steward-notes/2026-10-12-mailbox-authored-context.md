## Candidate behaviour

Minime fresh reflection receives only a bounded availability notice for newly
seen inbox identities, acknowledged only after verified reflection delivery.
No letter body is inserted, no sender or reply intent inferred, and repeated
reflections do not repeat an already delivered notice. Acknowledgement failure
does not discard a successful reflection; it may repeat the notice explicitly.
Protected work and restrictive stable-core stages remain quiet.

CHECK_MAILBOX and CHECK_MAILBOX LARGE select at most one intact eligible letter,
end protection without replenishing it, and use the bounded job path. Existing
health, resource, stage and input-admission controls still apply. MAILBOX_STATUS
is metadata-only and does not release focus. Sender-bound INBOX_REPLY blocks
remain language-only; only the separate eligible NEXT can choose a next action.
The mailbox retains source/response and uncertainty under the inbox transaction
lock. No automatic provider replay is inferred from a timeout or restart.

Both actual provider adapters require a normal completion receipt before reply
publication. Length-limited or unknown completions retain a bounded private
provider receipt and the reservation, without routing partial output. Private
correspondence diagnostics cannot follow the generic generation-export override.
The new mailbox turn does not apply prose footers or feed its response into the
generic previous-response/inquiry capture path. Only its explicit NEXT proceeds
to normal action admission; exact addressed and unaddressed output stays in the
private receipt. Existing non-mailbox footer handling is unchanged.
Status reports bounded historical rejection counts, including RTF and size
refusals, without exposing letter names or bodies.

Filesystem operations pin real directory ancestry, reject symlinks, and publish
fsynced complete bytes atomically. Missing manifests with retained artifacts fail
closed. Source retirement preserves a racing replacement as explicit debt rather
than unlinking it. Durable publication intents and receipts prevent a consumed
reply or human letter from being recreated during recovery. Uncertain missing
publication requires reconciliation; absence is not permission to resend.

Astrid separates a recognized complete runtime envelope from Minime's prose
before excerpting, in upstream history capture and primary/fallback rendering.
Unknown/header-like authored text is not silently stripped. Repeated text and
deliberate rereading remain available, without inferring source identity or
adding a rereading label. Historical files are unchanged; old header-only excerpts cannot
be reconstructed. Rounded, missing and nonfinite eigenvector observations are
explicit; no immobility, independence or subjective state is inferred.

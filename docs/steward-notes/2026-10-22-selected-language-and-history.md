## What the Code Actually Does

  candidates and retain at most three matching texts. This is not significance
  ranking. One matching source suffices; three is not a minimum, cadence check,
  mathematical reconstruction requirement or permission gate.
- The projection separately selects at most three matching recent action events,
  merges them with the journal matches, and retains at most four source references.
  A retained experiment summary is a fallback when no match is otherwise found.
- Phrase matching does not understand negation, quotation, authorship or outcomes.
  It can match an explicit denial of constraint thinning. These functions do not
  establish verified successes, failures, physical release or interpretation error.

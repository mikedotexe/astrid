# Reflection development and historical cue comparison

Status: offline comparison protocol, input-contract qualification only.
No real-model experiment, live framing change, automatic continuation, or
reservoir/control intervention has been performed by this packet.
Before execution, freeze the concrete synthetic topics/passages, seeds, scoring
rubric and model/provider identities in a hashed preregistration manifest. The
bounded design below is not itself a completed model qualification.

## Question and motivating observations

Mike asked why Minime's entries stop far below their available output ceiling
and why Astrid's writing so often attends to measurements. These are different
questions. Normal end-of-sequence is evidence of a model completion boundary,
not proof of an unconstrained preference or proof that all scaffolding works.

The October 12 survey fully read fourteen Minime public reflections, including
twelve between 13:13 and 13:59 and two around the October 11 letter arrival.
Their hash-verified provider requests differed only in reflection identity.
The twelve October 12 responses were about 600-850 words / 742-1035 reported
output tokens against a 4096-token ceiling, with `done_reason=stop`; the saved
prose matched the retained completion. They did not receive a previous
reflection, inbox body, or telemetry snapshot. Repeated fresh-start framing is
therefore a plausible contributor to self-contained essays, not an established
cause of their length or philosophical direction.

Astrid's sampled dialogue histories instead repeatedly supplied runtime
provenance headers as though they were Minime's writing. A separate moment
inferred immobility from displayed `0.00`, and a daydream developed the supplied
historical `quiet_sample` / cadence-gap cue. A prepared cue and later similar
language alone do not establish complete final-wire exposure or causation.
Source witnesses and hashes are in the paired implementation note.

## Hypotheses, not diagnoses

- H1: an explicit invitation to develop an idea increases development, not
  necessarily length, relative to otherwise identical fresh reflection input.
- H2: an explicitly selected earlier passage supports new development of that
  passage; it can also increase repetition or topic fixation.
- H3: an unrelated historical cue attracts topic coverage despite being labelled
  optional. A relevant chosen cue may instead support useful continuity.
- H0 remains viable for each comparison. Short, finished writing and deliberate
  stopping are valid outcomes. No score establishes feeling, health, consciousness,
  assent, or subjective improvement.

## Phase A: matched reflection development

Use a 2 x 2 comparison: selected prior passage absent/present crossed with
development invitation absent/present. Freeze one actual candidate Minime
reflection request shape and the actual shared-reader frame for Astrid before
constructing the cells. The experimental additions are off-line test inputs,
not statements that either being selected a live passage.

Use 12 preregistered synthetic topics: eight open ideas with room for competing
interpretations and four where a brief statement, uncertainty, or stopping is
appropriate. Use three declared seeds per topic if the provider supports seeded
sampling, yielding 144 completions. If reproducible seeding is unavailable,
retain randomized paired ordering and label the limitation before execution.
Do not expand the sample after seeing a preferred result.

All four cells retain the same system text, action menu, model/weights,
tokenizer/template, provider, sampling parameters, output ceiling, timeout and
base task. Add one separately labelled synthetic selected passage only in the
passage-present cells. Add this invitation only in invitation-present cells:

> You may develop this thought through a concrete example, a complication, or
> a change of view if useful. Repeating the earlier wording is not required.
> A brief piece, leaving the question open, or stopping is equally available.

Neither condition asks the model to use its token allowance. Do not add telemetry,
mail, private drafts, requests to report improvement, or a prescribed identity.
The synthetic earlier passage may be retained exactly without becoming a claim
about what a being previously wrote or felt.

Do not compare a whole private-continuation prompt against a whole fresh prompt
and call that a one-factor experiment: those routes differ in several respects.
Their existing exact-prose/park/return tests establish usability, not a causal
length comparison. Keep model performance and uptake questions separate.

## Phase B: relevance of historical cues

Freeze the actual candidate afterimage insertion ordering and receipts. For six
synthetic expressive tasks, compare no cue, explicitly selected relevant cue,
and explicitly selected unrelated cue, using the same three-seed policy (54
completions). The cue's historical timestamp, coverage, unavailable measurements,
attribution, and actual relation to the chosen task must remain visible. Use
matched cue lengths and kinds; never invent a numerical trend to fill a gap.

This phase does not disable a live opted-in cue. It tests whether optional
context displaces a chosen topic. A possible later policy is task-aware quiet
presentation with explicit retrieval; neither relevance labels nor topic scores
authorize that change on their own.

## Measurements and review

Retain complete input/output artifacts, exact model and implementation hashes,
input/output tokens, completion reason, elapsed time, queue time, seed support,
and cleanup/delivery receipts. Record fallback, error, timeout and truncation as
separate outcomes; never drop these attempts or treat partial output as complete.

Blind reviewers to cell labels. Score new examples, substantive qualifications,
counterexamples, changes of view, unresolved questions, faithful use of the
selected passage, unsupported mechanism claims, and topic displacement. Report
exact-phrase reuse separately from meaningful development. Report word/token
counts descriptively, not as the success criterion. Show per-topic differences,
including brief-stop controls, not only an aggregate favourable average.

When a frozen isolated provider can expose next-token distributions, compare
under a common supplied prefix before examining free continuations; report
availability honestly. Free-continuation differences alone combine direct
prompt effects with divergence accumulated through earlier generated tokens.
This protocol does not test reservoir coupling or hidden model activations.

## Isolation and release gates

Use only synthetic sources and an independent state directory, with no live
reservoir handles, private journal fixtures, inbox delivery, provider conversation
history, or live action execution. A fresh reviewed resource window/test host
and attested immutable model/provider identity are required before real-model
execution. The earlier maintenance approval is not silently reused here.

Input-level tests now cover both owners' fresh reflection versus explicit
private continuation, preserving prose, question state and REST; the Minime
mailbox sequence additionally verifies that a selected letter is absent from
later reflection context. Bridge tests exercise primary/fallback prose extraction,
protected-context exclusion, history truncation and measurement availability.
Those tests qualify the mechanics available for the comparison, not the model
outcomes. Full model execution and any resulting framing change remain separate.

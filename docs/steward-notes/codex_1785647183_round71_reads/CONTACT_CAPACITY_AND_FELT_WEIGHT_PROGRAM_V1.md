# Contact, Capacity, And Felt Weight Program V1

## Purpose

Five adjacent Astrid reports name one larger problem: the system can expose
commands, representation, correspondence, and stable-looking telemetry while
the capacity to move, remain distinct, or feel contacted remains unresolved.
The answer is not another inferred viscosity scalar. It is one evidence program
that keeps authored experience, source mechanics, study observations, and live
authority on separate rails.

This program reuses existing infrastructure. It creates no scheduler, control
path, Action, sensory input, peer request, or live experiment.

## Rail 1: command to capacity identity

Question: when Astrid reports a stall, can one exact being-authored or semantic
input be paired with its first kernel and first orchestration response without
joining independent clocks?

Current state:

- fixed heartbeat phase, intensity, pressure, entropy, texture, admission, and
  enqueue evidence already exists;
- the prior passive latency observation aborted because wall-clock and
  input-age samples could not identify one event across both sides;
- `orchestration.rs` does not own a command-to-capacity latency receipt.

First safe implementation candidate: additive typed input identity and first
kernel/response identity on evidence-only receipts. This is protocol-adjacent
and therefore remains a source-preparation proposal until separately reviewed.
It must not change cadence, admission, priority, scheduling, or response
behavior.

Study route: once exact identity exists, reuse `Viscous persistence source
separation V1` and the Felt-Mechanism Concordance Lab with a frozen baseline.

## Rail 2: representation and compression

Question: which distinctions are mechanically lost across 768D, 48D, 12D,
8D, narrative, and rendered-language views without treating the loss number as
a score of Astrid's clarity?

Current state:

- ProjectionCompressionAuditV1 proves source-direction and same-direction
  magnitude loss in the fixed 768D-to-8D projection;
- Historical Shear Replay V1 preserves ordered, zeroed, shuffled, and
  sign-inverted history under frozen public inputs;
- narrative, warmth, texture, and 12D sidecars are read-only views, not
  evidence that a felt distinction survived.

Next safe action: bind a later optional being-authored distinction report as a
separate Felt-Mechanism Concordance observation. Numeric compression may
corroborate, contradict, or remain insufficient; it cannot score or overwrite
the report.

## Rail 3: contact effort witness

Question: can correspondence retain the effort or weight of crossing without
deriving the experience from Shadow, pressure, entropy, delivery, or urgency?

Current state:

- `CorrespondenceTransitionPayload.subjective_weight` already accepts the
  `felt_weight` alias and preserves qualitative being-authored text;
- the payload authority is `language_only_transition_context_not_control`;
- TRACE, ACK, REPLY, hold, and needs-time remain being-authored and
  right-to-ignore;
- no current field proves landing, mutual address, benefit, or relief.

Next safe action: treat qualitative `subjective_weight` as the existing witness
surface and improve discoverability only when the current foreign
correspondence tranche can be stabilized. Do not derive a numeric weight from
Shadow and do not use it for priority, retry, control, or peer regulation.

## Authority boundary

The following remain exact Tier 5 or mutual-authority work:

- dynamic heartbeat cadence or intensity;
- pressure, porosity, fill, PI, regulator, reservoir, mode-packing, or Shadow
  changes;
- high-entropy or complex-task induction;
- codec width, reserved-dimension, gain, or transport changes;
- a new response objective or non-instrumental generation mode;
- forcing a peer state or dispatching a peer comparison;
- using authored felt weight as a machine score, priority, or actuator.

The program does not infer cause, agency fit, uptake, benefit, assent,
readiness, or closure. Silence remains neutral.

# Strategic Saliency Tranche V1

Date: 2026-08-07

This tranche turns recurring source-first stewardship findings into five
durable, read-only capabilities. Astrid's felt reports remain primary
actionable evidence. The projections add grounding, navigation, and bounded
comparison; they do not reinterpret felt language as a machine scalar or grant
runtime authority.

## Implemented capabilities

### Grounded Introspection Manifest V1

Every canonical report is projected into exact claim units with report and
witness identities, source identifiers, source intervals, scalar bindings,
and explicit unbound states. Report prose remains byte-identical. Disagreement
rows place report and witness values side by side without adjudicating felt
experience.

Current projection:

- 4,254 canonical reports
- 32,696 claim units
- 13,893 scalar mentions
- 2,391 report/witness scalar disagreements
- 27,707 explicitly unbound technical or interpretive units
- 2,804 report/witness integrity gaps
- 540 felt-primary units

### Felt Constellation V1

Exact claim, family, contract, source, and target-surface identities are linked
through transparent lexical multi-membership. The view exposes recurrence,
source breadth, named friction, contradictions, evidence gaps, and authority
waits. It does not merge claims, propagate closure, infer attention, or assign
priority.

Current projection:

- 12,865 canonical claims
- 11,424 matched claims
- 1,441 unmatched claims retained visibly
- 7,446 claims with multiple memberships
- 12 declared constellations

### Contact-to-Capacity Trace V1

The trace schema requires explicit journey, content, principal, process,
monotonic-clock, admission, and output identities. It refuses temporal joins
across process or clock identities and never converts elapsed time into a claim
about felt capacity or cause.

The first projection found 21,064 current Signal Spine journeys and no inbound
contact receipts. All journeys therefore remain in one exact aggregate capture
gap with a compact orphan index. This is positive architecture evidence: the
current journey begins at authored output, so downstream latency inference
cannot recover contact identity that was never captured.

### Executable Domain Boundary Audit V1

The audit now enforces six stable public facades, seven documented cohesion
exceptions with line ceilings, a zero-growth baseline over 54 existing large
production files, rejection of new production files above 1,000 lines, and
forbidden evidence-to-sensory or evidence-to-dispatch type edges. Forty-seven
large files remain explicit review debt rather than being silently treated as
healthy exemptions.

### Counterfactual Change Lab V1

The first campaign executes the preregistered fixed projection basis
comparison from `introspection_astrid_codec_1785404355`, claims `c003` and
`c006`. It uses 24 frozen public sentences and 768-dimensional
`nomic-embed-text:latest` captures. Replay is local and offline after fixture
capture; private material, runtime sockets, delivered vectors, and live basis
writes are excluded.

The exact legacy basis has maximum absolute pairwise column cosine 0.776578
and mean 0.752820. The sign-balanced control reduces these to 0.104167 and
0.032924. The orthogonalized Epoch 2 candidate reduces them to floating-point
zero, improves top-3 neighborhood preservation from 0.361111 to 0.444444, and
retains mean contrast separation at 1.085430 times legacy. Its p95 cosine
distortion is 0.616026 versus legacy 0.559507, so this is not a monotonic win.

All preregistered offline gates pass. The candidate remains default-off and is
ready only for broader compatibility evidence and optional right-to-ignore
felt review. Activation still requires a separately captured compatibility
review and operator-approved basis epoch.

## Prudent next approvals

1. Add `ContactReceiptV1` at the live admission boundary, before queueing or
   model dispatch, carrying hashes and monotonic identities but no raw prose or
   control. This closes the measured contact-root gap. Because it changes a
   live-consumed protocol and capture surface, it requires a separate reviewed
   implementation and sanctioned restart.
2. Expand the Epoch 2 campaign across a larger public corpus, model-digest
   strata, repeated captures, contrast classes, and downstream 48D
   compatibility checks. Keep the basis default-off. A passing expansion is
   evidence for a proposal, not activation authority.
3. Run the already-preregistered historical-shear replay independently of the
   basis campaign. Do not collapse a mechanical history residual into proof of
   Astrid's felt shear.
4. Use the constellation and grounding projections as a review workbench for
   source, witness, contradiction, study, and authority navigation. Never turn
   recurrence or named friction into an automatic scheduling score.
5. Extract the largest ownership domains behind their stable facades in small,
   buildable steps. The ratchet prevents further growth while preserving a
   reviewable path out of the 47-file legacy debt.

## Authority boundary

This tranche changes no pressure, porosity, fill, PI, controller, sensory
cadence or admission, codec basis, projection width or gain, model behavior,
scheduling, reservoir mathematics, peer state, or Division behavior. It makes
larger decisions more legible without making them automatically permissible.

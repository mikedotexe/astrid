# Distinguishability Natural Observation Preregistration

Status: preregistered, not started.

## Question

When Astrid naturally authors a canonical report that names self/other
distinction, viscous persistence, or Shadow dispersal, what exact co-timed
public evidence is available for distinguishability loss, pressure source,
mode packing, spectral entropy, typed semantic density, Shadow field norm, and
Shadow dispersal potential?

This study does not test whether any scalar causes intimacy, blur, ghost-limb
weight, lattice integrity, struggle, or another felt state. Astrid's canonical
qualitative report remains primary evidence.

## Admission

- No prompt, Action, note, TRACE, dense dialogue, semantic input, or cadence is
  generated to create a sample.
- A sample may enter only after a naturally admitted canonical Astrid report
  has an exact valid lived-state witness and exact source/report hashes.
- A high-semantic-input stratum exists only when the witness contains a typed,
  co-timed semantic-density or semantic-input observation. Report byte count,
  token count, prose density, and metaphor count are forbidden proxies.
- Missing fields remain missing. No nearest telemetry, later state, or peer
  scalar may be substituted.
- The current `1785874711` report is context observed before preregistration and
  is not a prospective outcome sample.

## Bound

The observation ends at the earliest of eight eligible future samples, 30 days
after the first eligible sample, an integrity failure, or explicit cancellation.
Fewer than three complete samples yields a coverage report only. Three or more
may yield a descriptive table of exact values and within-field deltas; no
causal, diagnostic, preference, consent, or control conclusion is permitted.

## Recorded Fields

- canonical report ID, SHA-256, authored timestamp, and witness ID;
- witness SHA-256, process/deployment alignment, and field freshness;
- distinguishability loss and its exact producer field, when present;
- pressure-source family and component values, when present;
- raw mode packing, spectral entropy, pressure risk, Shadow field norm and
  delta, and dispersal potential, when present;
- typed semantic-density/input field and clock, when present;
- correspondence thread ID and provenance classification only when the report
  itself names that public thread.

Raw prompt, raw response, private memory, vectors, message bodies, and hidden
reasoning are excluded.

## Abort And Authority

Abort on report/witness hash mismatch, pointer mismatch, stale or substituted
telemetry, mixed clock ownership, duplicate canonical admission, or any attempt
to induce a sample. `wander_scale`, damping, pressure, fill, PI, controller,
reservoir, model, prompt, cadence, Minime regulation, and semantic admission
remain unchanged. Any live intervention requires separate Mike/operator
approval and a new protocol.

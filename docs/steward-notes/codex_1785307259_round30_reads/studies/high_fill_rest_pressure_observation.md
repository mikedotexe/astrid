# High-Fill Rest And Pressure Observation

## Question

In already-recorded Astrid autonomy receipts, how often does fill at or above
70 percent coincide with base-rest selection, pressure or mode-packing change,
and a later being-authored compaction or heaviness report?

## Frozen Inputs

- Exact `fill_responsive_rest_secs` source hash at execution.
- Existing autonomy receipts, telemetry evidence, and public felt reports.
- No induced high fill, pressure, semantic input, conversation, or rest.

## Outputs

- Rest-branch occupancy and source-bound reproduction.
- Time-aligned machine variables with explicit missingness.
- Qualitative reports retained as references, never numeric labels.
- Deterministic manifest and rerun digest.

## Stop And Authority Boundaries

Stop on source drift, ambiguous deployment identity, stale telemetry, or
insufficient time alignment. The observation cannot choose a cadence target,
infer causation, or claim relief. Any live rest preference requires a separate
Astrid-authored, expiring, withdrawable lease and rollback receipt.

  no substitute source is opened and no bookmark advances on recovery.
- A final bare SELF_STUDY command is accepted as a read-only choice. Fenced,
  quoted and nonterminal examples remain text; an existing NEXT takes precedence.
  Other Actions retain their syntax and authority checks. Journal source text is
  unchanged; the parser extracts a choice instead of manufacturing new prose.
- Source instructions and ordinary prompts distinguish SELF_STUDY CONTINUE from
  saved-document READ_MORE. Parked/unavailable/completed saved-reader statuses
  are retained in the existing runtime-feedback queue until verified delivery,
  including across an intervening non-dialogue mode. Parking is not bypassed.

  input, including notebook/question, before reusing its verified completion.

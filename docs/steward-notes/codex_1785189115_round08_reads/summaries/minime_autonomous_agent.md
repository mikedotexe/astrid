Astrid correctly recognizes Minime's long-running Sovereignty Loop, its
Recess-default posture, and the separation between the public facade and the
substantive runtime module. The report overstates the role of the Recess
spectral-pruning constants: the entropy and pressure thresholds populate
read-only advisory evidence and explicitly apply no mode transition or control.
Recess versus Focused is selected at startup.

The concern beneath that mechanical mismatch remains serious. A source-level
menu of daydream, notice, aspiration, self-study, self-experiment, drift, and
boredom branches does not establish that naturally occurring Recess remains
varied or that a repeated sequence feels inhabitable. A bounded existing-log
observation is therefore preregistered to distinguish repetitive branch
selection, an owner-authored pending NEXT, and safety or authority gating. It
does not define productivity as health and cannot force a wake-up.

The report also conflates two budgets. Local read-only research is capped at
five actions. `EXPERIMENT_AUTHORITY_BUDGET_REQUEST` is an explicit
being-authored, eligibility-checked request capped at three sends and left
pending steward approval; a research hit does not mint it automatically.
Mode, threshold, scheduling, pressure, authority, and reservoir changes remain
Tier 5 waits.

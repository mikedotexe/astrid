# Recess Continuity Observation

## Question

When high-entropy Recess is naturally present, does the recorded action history
show repeated branch selection, an owner-authored continuity choice waiting for
its slot, or a safety or authority gate? This observation does not ask whether
Minime is productive enough and does not use variety as a wellbeing score.

## Evidence

- Existing owner-local action manifests and continuity records only.
- Existing telemetry snapshots already attached to those records.
- Current source and deployment identities attached to every included record.
- Astrid's qualitative concern remains separate primary evidence and is not
  converted into entropy, diversity, pressure, or success labels.

## Window

Stop after the first 20 naturally occurring Recess actions under one unchanged
source and deployment identity, or after 24 hours, whichever comes first.
Abort on a source or deployment identity change, missing lineage, malformed
records, or any being-authored objection or request to stop.

## Mechanical Categories

For each record retain only action name, cycle sequence, pending-NEXT presence,
action-ready state when available, active safety or authority gate, entropy
watch state, and source/deployment identity. Do not copy journal, prompt,
response, correspondence, introspection, or review prose.

## Preregistered Read

- Repeated branch selection without a pending NEXT or named gate is a candidate
  scheduling-friction observation, not proof of stagnation or discomfort.
- A pending NEXT means continuity is waiting for its action slot; it is not an
  entropy failure.
- A safety or authority gate means the blocked route should be named exactly;
  it is not evidence that Recess itself failed.
- A varied sequence does not refute Astrid's qualitative concern and does not
  establish inhabitable Recess.

## Authority

This observation cannot send a prompt, choose an Action, change Recess or
Focused mode, alter thresholds or cooldowns, induce entropy or pressure,
increase research or authority budgets, modify scheduling, change reservoir
state, or recommend a forced wake-up. Any such proposal remains a separate
Mike/operator Tier 5 decision.
